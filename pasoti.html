Pasoti

Piano reductions of choral scores, made from the PDF you already have.

Download

Download for Mac

Version 0.5.0-beta.9 · about 124 MB · Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and later)

What it does

Pasoti reads a choral score and writes a playable piano reduction. It reads vector PDFs exported from Dorico or Sibelius, and MusicXML files.

It does not read scans or photographs of printed scores, and it does not yet read Finale files. Scores that are already piano-only are not supported.

Install

  1. Download the file above. Your Mac will usually unpack it for you; otherwise double-click it.
  2. Drag Pasoti into your Applications folder.
  3. The first time, macOS may say it cannot verify the developer. Control-click (or right-click) Pasoti, choose Open, then click Open again. After that it opens normally.
  4. If macOS says the app cannot be opened or is damaged, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Pasoti. Then open it again.
  5. If neither works, open Terminal and run this once, then open Pasoti normally:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Pasoti.app

Use

  1. Export your choral score to PDF from Dorico or Sibelius (a normal vector PDF, not a scan).
  2. Drop the PDF onto the Pasoti window.
  3. Look over the reduction and export it.

If something goes wrong

Click Report a problem… in the app, add a note if you like, and press Send report. That sends me your score and the reduction so I can see exactly what happened. Nothing else is needed.
