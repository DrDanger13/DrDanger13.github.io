Piano & Orchestra

Cryptidology

A Concerto for Piano and Orchestra
2026 Solo piano and full orchestra Premiere: Bemidji Symphony Orchestra, Beverly Everett, cond.; Ann DuHamel, piano — May 2026

Cryptidology draws on the stories and folklore of my native West Virginia. The first movement, Mothman Commeth, is an exploration of timbre and color built around the Mothman legend of Point Pleasant; the second, Zona's Lullaby, takes up the 1897 case of Zona Heaster Shue, the only known U.S. murder conviction aided by testimony said to come from a ghost.

Two Vestiges

for chamber orchestra
2009–2010 12′ Sinfonietta Premiere: Studio New Music Ensemble, Moscow Conservatory, Igor Dronov, cond. — April 24, 2010 Indianapolis Chamber Orchestra Composition Prize, 2010

Two movements, each a sonic reading of an artistic or spatial design encountered in Europe: Ribbon Sculpture, after the work of Ursula Burghardt, weaves intertwining lines that collide and dissolve; Japanese Garden (karesansui) slows perception to the stillness of a dry landscape garden, with rippling textures around stark solo colors.

The Solids

for spatialized clarinet ensemble
2017 12′ I. Cube · II. Tetrahedron · III. Icosahedron Premiere: University of Florida, April 4, 2017

Geometric form and physical mass translated into sound. Firmly rooted pitch centers and unyielding motivic blocks act as the “solids,” set against fleeting passages that move around them like light across sculpture, so that the music is heard as a space to be inhabited as much as a line through time.

Dreams of Fin

for violin and piano
2006 8′ Commissioned by Wolfgang David Recorded by Wolfgang David & David Gompper, Albany Records

A musical dialogue with David Gompper's Finnegan's Wake, taking its cues from Joyce's dream world. Violin and piano act as equal partners in a dense, atmospheric texture, moving between driving virtuosity and fragile, suspended stillness.

Solo

Remnants

of time and space, for piano
2009 9′ Seven movements in two parts

Written while I was living in Russia, Remnants is a travelogue of two places: Moscow and the vast, silent expanse of Lake Baikal. Part I concerns time, movement, and physical space, its continuum broken in the third movement by a descending gesture carried over from the second; Part II turns to the fluctuations and atmosphere of the Siberian landscape.

MicroSuite

for organ
2009 8′ Commissioned by Carson Cooman

An experiment in form: five distinct dance forms drawn from the Baroque suite, each reduced to its essentials and compressed into a single, tightly knit movement. Dense registration changes are set against sudden minimalist restraint.
