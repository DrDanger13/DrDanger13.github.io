Winter Rites

concertino for violoncello and ensemble
Composer's Note

Winter Rites is a brooding, visceral exploration of seasonal transformation, primal ritual, and physical endurance. Written for chamber ensemble, the piece approaches winter not merely as a passive landscape of cold and silence, but as an active, elemental force; a season of stark reduction, transformation, and ancient ceremony.

Structurally, Winter Rites unfolds through stark contrasts in timbre and density. Dangerfield constructs an atmospheric world using brittle string techniques, dark, resonance-heavy piano sonorities, and razor-sharp woodwind gestures. Fragments of ritualistic motifs continuously emerge, fracture, and reconfigure, creating a sense of relentless forward motion beneath an icy, suspended surface.

The work balances moments of brittle, crystalline stillness with sudden, violent outbursts of rhythmic energy. As the ensemble navigates these shifting textures, Winter Rites captures the tension between the harsh isolation of the winter landscape and the intense, driving impulse toward renewal that lies beneath it.
