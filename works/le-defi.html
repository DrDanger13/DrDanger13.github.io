Le Défi

for saxophone and piano
Program Note

Le Défi (The Challenge) is an exploration of musical tension, confrontation, and ultimate resolution. The work balances technical rigor with expressive depth, crafting a visceral listening experience that challenges both the performers’ limits and the audience’s expectations.

The title itself serves as the foundational concept for the piece. Structurally, Le Défi unfolds as a series of sonic trials, frequently pitting opposing musical forces against one another. These contrasting elements—ranging from stark gestures to dense textures—do not merely coexist; they actively duel, generating a kinetic energy that drives the narrative forward. Through the use of textures and carefully calibrated dynamics, the work stretches the expressive capabilities of the instruments, transforming the physical act of performance into a thematic struggle.

The work was commissioned by Michael Ibrahim and the saxophone studio at West Virginia University, as their piece de concours for the 2014–15 academic year.
