Knot

for electric flute, electric cello, and electric piano
Program Note

Knot was conceived inherently as a companion piece to George Crumb’s landmark 1971 masterpiece Vox Balaenae (Voice of the Whale), and is based on the Celtic Zodiac. Knot utilizes and mirrors the extended instrumental techniques and unique timbral structures popularized by Crumb’s work.

The piece explores a sound world that stretches the boundaries of standard instrumentation. Listeners will hear the performers’ manipulation of tone and texture, which includes unique timbral effects such as the flutist utilizing specialized air-stream techniques to produce a haunting, hollow resonance akin to subterranean environmental calls or the songs of marine mammals. Through its intricate, tightly wound harmonic lines and percussive, localized gestures, Knot weaves a complex tapestry that mimics the tension and eventual release of its namesake structure, creating a deeply immersive auditory experience.
