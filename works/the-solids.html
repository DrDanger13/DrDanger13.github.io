Chamber

The Solids

for spatialized clarinet ensemble
Premiered at University of Florida — April 4, 2017
Program Note

The Solids functions as an extension of a lifelong fascination with translating tactile, spatial dimensions into abstract soundscapes. Taking its conceptual cues from both geometric forms and the rigid properties of the physical world, the piece acts as an acoustic exploration of structure, mass, and negative space.

Musically, the composition is built around the friction between static immobility and explosive momentum. Dangerfield treats his musical cells as physical materials—carving them out through stark instrumentations, dense harmonic clusters, and sudden, chiseled rhythmic interruptions. The “solids” of the title manifest as firmly rooted pitch centers and unyielding motivic blocks that anchor the ensemble. Surrounding these dense structures are fluid, fleeting passages that mimic the way light shifts across a physical sculpture, exposing new textures, hidden depths, and unexpected vulnerabilities within the form.

Ultimately, The Solids challenges the listener to perceive music not just as a linear progression through time, but as an architectural space to be inhabited. Through a meticulous balance of form and kinetic energy, Dangerfield constructs a sonic monument that is as intellectually rigorous as it is viscerally compelling.
